Home/Privacy Policy
This website is a static informational website. It does not operate a database or backend that stores form submissions on our servers.

1. Introduction

तीर्था झेरॉक्स व डिजिटल सेवा केंद्र, अंबप ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information shared through website enquiries may be used when you contact us.

2. Information Collected Through Enquiries

When you use the contact form, your browser opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message containing details you enter, such as:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Selected service
  • Message / enquiry details

These details are shared with us only when you send the WhatsApp message. The website itself does not store this information on a server.

3. Use of Customer Contact Information

Information you share through call, email or WhatsApp may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Provide service-related guidance
  • Follow up on application assistance requests
  • Share practical next steps related to your requirement

We do not sell customer contact information.

4. Data Protection

We take reasonable care to handle enquiry communications responsibly. Because this website does not host a customer database, enquiry records are limited to communication channels you choose (such as WhatsApp, phone or email).

5. Third-Party Services

This website may use or link to third-party platforms such as:

  • WhatsApp
  • Google Maps
  • Font and icon CDNs
  • Government or service portals during application assistance (outside this website)

Those platforms have their own privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies when using their services.

6. Cookies

This static website does not currently implement custom tracking cookies or analytics cookies from our side. Third-party embedded content (for example Google Maps) may set cookies according to their own policies.

7. Documents Shared for Services

If you share personal documents for application assistance at our center or over messaging, such documents should be shared only as needed for the requested service. Final processing happens through the concerned portals or departments.
